THIRD-PARTY COMPONENTS

1. Computational engine
   The Avaris2 engine is built on the EPA Storm Water Management Model (SWMM)
   version 5.2.4, developed by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. The
   SWMM source code is in the public domain. Avaris modifications include:
     - user-configurable dynamic-wave under-relaxation (UNDER_RELAXATION)
     - OpenMP parallel node-depth solution with per-node convergence flags
     - extended "Most Frequent Nonconverging Nodes" report (NONCONV_REPORT_COUNT)
     - outfalls excluded from node nonconvergence statistics
     - non-SWMM input sections (e.g. Avaris DS [GROUPS]) are skipped instead of failing
